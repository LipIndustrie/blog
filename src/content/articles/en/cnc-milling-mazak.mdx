Milling is one of our core strengths. At LIP Industrie Précision, our milling department runs
**three CNC machining centres** with radically different capabilities: pure 5-axis milling on
the Variaxis, continuous 5-axis turn/mill on the Integrex J-200, and large-format machining on
the Nexus 510C. Each machine is operated by a dedicated technician who knows it in detail.

## Mazak Variaxis i-500 — continuous 5-axis milling

<Image src={imgVariaxisFace} alt="Mazak Variaxis 5-axis machining centre front view with Smooth Technology control — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mazak Variaxis i-500 — 5-axis machining centre, LIP Industrie Précision" />

The **Mazak Variaxis i-500** is our **continuous 5-axis machining centre**. Simultaneous A and C
axes: the spindle tilts and the table rotates during cutting, with no repositioning.

<Image src={imgVariaxisInterieur} alt="Interior of the Mazak Variaxis 5-axis machining centre with tilted spindle head and rotary table — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mazak Variaxis i-500 in production — 5-axis head, LIP Industrie Précision" />

Key specifications:

- **X/Y/Z travel**: 356 × 559 × 508 mm
- **Spindle speed**: 12,000 rpm
- **Tool magazine**: 30 positions
- **Simultaneous axes**: 5-axis A+C continuous interpolation
- **Rapid traverse**: 60 m/min

Continuous 5-axis fundamentally changes what can be produced: complex surfaces in a single
setup, with no repositioning error between phases. It is the natural machine for freeform
geometry, impellers, undercuts, or complex prismatic parts that 3-axis would require multiple
refixturings to complete.

## Mazak Integrex J-200 — 5-axis turn/mill

<Image src={imgIntegrexFace} alt="Mazak Integrex J-200 Smooth Technology multi-tasking centre front view with control — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mazak Integrex J-200 — 5-axis turn/mill centre, LIP Industrie Précision" />

The **Integrex J-200** belongs to a different category: it is neither a lathe nor a machining
centre — it is both simultaneously. It combines a **turning spindle** with a **milling spindle
on a continuous B-axis**, able to approach the part from any angle while it rotates.

<Image src={imgIntegrexMagazin} alt="Integrex J-200 tool magazine with HSK toolholders visible through the window — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mazak Integrex J-200 — tool magazine, LIP Industrie Précision" />

Key specifications:

- **Max. turning diameter**: Ø 500 mm
- **Machinable length**: 500 mm
- **Milling spindle speed**: 5,000 rpm
- **Tool magazine**: 20 positions
- **B-axis**: continuous (orientable milling head)

In practice: a raw blank enters the Integrex, gets turned, then milled at any angle, drilled,
tapped — all in the same clamping. What would otherwise require two machines and two setups
(lathe then machining centre) is done in a single cycle. The gain is not only time: it is also
the accuracy of dimensions that relate across turning and milling operations, guaranteed by the
absence of reclamping.

## Mazak Nexus 510C — large table

<Image src={imgNexus} alt="Mazak Nexus 510C vertical machining centre with control panel — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mazak Nexus 510C — large-table machining centre, LIP Industrie Précision" />

The **Mazak Nexus 510C** is our 3-axis machining centre for parts that require a large clamping
surface. Its **1,300 × 550 mm table** and **1,200 kg load capacity** accommodate large parts or
multi-part fixtures that the Variaxis cannot take.

Key specifications:

- **X/Y/Z travel**: 1,050 × 510 × 510 mm
- **Table**: 1,300 × 550 mm — 1,200 kg capacity
- **Spindle speed**: 12,000 rpm
- **Tool magazine**: 30 positions

The Nexus is the go-to machine for plates, large flanges, bodies, long guide surfaces. Its large
table also allows multiple parts to be clamped simultaneously in production, reducing changeover
time between batches.

## Three machines, three specialities — one dedicated technician per machine

One of the strengths of our milling organisation: each machine is assigned to a technician who
knows it intimately. No generic rotation — each operator masters the subtleties of their
machine: thermal behaviour, tool wear patterns, optimal programming strategies, limits to
respect. This specialisation translates directly into part quality and the ability to hold tight
tolerances in production.

In practice:

- The **Variaxis** handles complex-geometry parts requiring simultaneous 5-axis: freeform
  surfaces, multi-angle features, parts to be machined on several faces in one setup.
- The **Integrex J-200** is the natural choice for parts combining turning and milling —
  complex rotational parts with milled pockets or features on turned surfaces.
- The **Nexus 510C** covers large footprints and multi-part production batches.

## What our fleet delivers

- **Complex 5-axis parts**: freeform surfaces, impellers, multi-angle geometry in one setup.
- **Combined turn/mill** on the Integrex: a single clamping for parts that mix rotation and milling.
- **Large parts** up to 1,050 mm machinable length and 1,200 kg.
- **All materials**: steel, stainless, aluminium, titanium, bronze, copper, engineering plastics.
- **Single units to production runs**: the three centres cover the full volume range.

All parts inspected in-house on our [CMM and roughness tester](/en/articles/quality-control-metrology/).
