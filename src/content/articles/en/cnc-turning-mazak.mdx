CNC turning is at the heart of our operations. At LIP Industrie Précision, our turning department
runs **three CNC lathes** organised to cover the full spectrum: from complex one-off parts to
automated production runs. Two recent Mazak machines deliver precision and versatility; a Mori
Seiki with bar feeder handles bar-stock series.

## Mazak QTE-100M SG — compact and fully equipped

<Image src={imgQTE100interieur} alt="Interior of the Mazak QTE-100M SG CNC lathe with driven-tool turret and tailstock — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mazak QTE-100M SG — CNC lathe with driven tools, LIP Industrie Précision" />

The **Mazak QTE-100M SG** is our compact CNC lathe, running Mazak's **Smooth Technology**
control. Despite its smaller footprint, it carries a **driven-tool turret** and a **tailstock**
— enabling milling, drilling and threading off-centreline without unclamping the part.

Key specifications:

- **Max. diameter**: Ø 300 mm
- **Max. length**: 500 mm
- **Y-axis**: ±52 mm (off-axis milling)
- **Spindle speed**: 6,000 rpm
- **Driven tools**: milling, drilling, threading in a single setup

This is the natural machine for small and medium-format parts requiring multiple operations —
a raw blank can leave as a turned, milled and drilled finished part in a single cycle.

## Mazak Quick Turn 250MY — large-format versatility

<Image src={imgQT250face} alt="Mazak Quick Turn 250MY CNC lathe with door open, turret and tailstock visible — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mazak Quick Turn 250MY — large-capacity CNC lathe with Y-axis, LIP Industrie Précision" />

The **Mazak Quick Turn 250MY** is our reference machine for longer and larger-diameter parts.
Same philosophy as the QTE-100M SG — Y-axis, driven tools, tailstock — but in a significantly
larger format.

<Image src={imgQT250copeaux} alt="Interior of the Mazak Quick Turn 250MY during machining with bronze chips — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mazak Quick Turn 250MY in production — bronze turning, LIP Industrie Précision" />

Key specifications:

- **Max. diameter**: Ø 300 mm
- **Max. length**: 1,524 mm
- **Y-axis**: ±52 mm
- **Spindle speed**: 5,000 rpm
- **Driven tools** + **tailstock**

With over 1.5 metres of machinable length, the 250MY handles long shafts, cylinder bodies
and drive axles — parts that few compact lathes can reach. Its Y-axis allows flats, grooves
and off-axis bores to be milled directly after turning, in the same setup.

## Two complementary Mazak machines

<Image src={imgAtelier} alt="Turning workshop at LIP Industrie Précision: Mazak Quick Turn 250MY in the foreground and QTE-100M SG in the background" title="CNC turning fleet — Mazak Quick Turn 250MY and QTE-100M SG, LIP Industrie Précision Besançon" />

Both machines share the same **Mazak Smooth Technology** control — simplifying programming
and allowing a program to be transferred from one machine to the other when capacity is needed.
In practice:

- The **QTE-100M SG** handles short parts and small formats with high responsiveness.
- The **Quick Turn 250MY** takes over when length or diameter exceeds the QTE's range, or when
  the part requires tight tolerances over long bearing surfaces.

## Mori Seiki — bar-stock production

<Image src={imgMoriSeiki} alt="Mori Seiki CNC lathe with automatic bar feeder and Fanuc control — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mori Seiki with bar feeder — automated series turning, LIP Industrie Précision" />

Our third lathe is a **Mori Seiki** fitted with an **automatic bar feeder**. Its purpose is
different: not complexity, but **throughput**.

<Image src={imgMoriPiece} alt="Interior of the Mori Seiki with part being turned — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mori Seiki in series production — LIP Industrie Précision" />

With the bar feeder, the machine runs autonomously through production batches, feeding parts
directly from bar stock — no operator intervention between parts. It is the logical choice for
**medium production runs of simpler parts**: precision fasteners, bushings, inserts, connector
parts. While the Mori Seiki runs a series, the Mazak machines remain available for complex or
urgent work.

## The expertise that makes the difference

A CNC machine is only as good as the operator who programs and runs it. Our turning department
manager, **F.F.**, has mastered his machines over many years — including their use alongside
coordinate measurement to validate his own setups. This autonomy from setup through to
inspection is rare: it means detecting and correcting drift mid-series without waiting for a
separate metrology pass, ensuring consistent quality from the first to the last part.

> "On the Mazaks, with the Y-axis and driven tools, you can deliver a complete part in a single
> setup. It completely changes the precision you achieve on dimensions that relate across
> different faces."
>
> — F.F., turning department manager at LIP Industrie Précision
