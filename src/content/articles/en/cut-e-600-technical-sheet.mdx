The **Cut E 600** by AgieCharmilles (+GF+) is our **large-travel** wire EDM machine.
While most wire-cutting machines are limited to relatively modest part sizes, the Cut E 600
accepts formats and masses that very few subcontracting workshops can handle.
Here are its full capabilities.

## Travel that changes everything

<Image src={imgFlanc} alt="Side view of the AgieCharmilles Cut E 600 with its engraved name — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="AgieCharmilles Cut E 600 — large-travel wire EDM, LIP Industrie Précision" />

The Cut E 600 offers a travel range of **600 × 400 × 300 mm** (X × Y × Z). In practice,
this means it can machine large parts — oversized dies, tool blocks, structural components —
without repositioning or multi-pass splitting.

To put it in perspective: with 600 mm of X travel, we machine lengths that many CNC machining
centres could not reach in a single pass.

## Table load: up to 1,000 kg

This is one of the machine's most remarkable features: **the table supports up to 1,000 kg**.
This opens the door to massive workpieces — hardened steel blocks, heavy dies, structural
components — that would simply be impossible to mount on a standard machine.

Combined with the large travel, this load capacity makes the Cut E 600 the go-to machine
whenever a part exceeds the common format.

## Micron-level precision

<Image src={imgFace} alt="Front view of the Cut E 600 +GF+ AgieCharmilles in the LIP Industrie Précision workshop in Besançon" title="Cut E 600 front view — wire EDM LIP Industrie Précision" />

Despite its size, the Cut E 600 does not compromise on precision. Like any high-end wire EDM
machine, it works to **tolerances in the order of a micron** and delivers **clean surface
finishes**, suited to the demands of the medical, aerospace and space sectors.

Wire EDM generates **no mechanical force** on the part: no stress, no distortion. A
heat-treated, fragile or thin part remains stable throughout the entire machining cycle — something
that no conventional material-removal process can guarantee.

## What the Cut E 600 machines

Thanks to its travel and load capacity, it is particularly suited to:

- **Large dies and tooling**: blanking dies, forming tools, moulds.
- **Long structural parts**: profiles, connecting rods, mechanism arms.
- **Massive hardened steel blocks**: weight no longer limits feasibility.
- **Complex parts in a single setup**: no repositioning, no accumulated error.

## And the Cut F 350?

Our fleet also includes a **[Cut F 350](/en/articles/cut-f-350-technical-sheet)**, a newer machine
oriented towards pure performance: higher cutting speed, even finer surface finish, increased precision.

The two machines are complementary: the Cut E 600 handles **large parts and heavy loads**,
while the Cut F 350 maximises **surface quality and productivity** on standard formats.
