The **Cut F 350** is our most recent wire EDM machine. Compared to the
[Cut E 600](/en/articles/cut-e-600-technical-sheet), it is not here to gain in format —
it is here to **gain in performance**. A more modern generator, a more precise structure,
integrated thermo-stabilisation: here is what sets it apart.

## A next-generation generator

<Image src={imgMachine} alt="Cut F 350 +GF+ AgieCharmilles with its wire spool and interface — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Cut F 350 AgieCharmilles — high-performance wire EDM, LIP Industrie Précision" />

The heart of a wire EDM machine is its **generator**: it drives the electrical discharges —
their frequency, their energy, the way they sequence. The Cut F 350 carries a latest-generation
generator, more refined in its pulse management.

Concrete results:

- **Higher cutting speed**: the generator optimises discharge sequencing, which directly
  translates into a higher material removal rate.
- **Better surface finish**: better-controlled pulses leave a smoother surface with lower
  roughness (Ra) — a decisive advantage for medical device parts, precision connectors or any
  component where surface finish is specified.

## High-quality motion axes

<Image src={imgDetail} alt="Detail of the Cut F 350 in operation, +GF+ logo illuminated — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Cut F 350 in operation — wire EDM LIP Industrie Précision" />

The precision of a machine tool also depends on the quality of its movements. The Cut F 350 is
equipped with **high-quality ballscrews** that move the frame: reduced backlash, improved
repeatability, smooth movement even on complex trajectories.

This is a detail that matters greatly in wire EDM, where positioning precision directly affects
the geometry of the machined part.

## Thermo-stabilised scales: precision that holds over time

This is probably the most significant technical feature of the Cut F 350 compared to a
standard machine: its **measuring scales are thermo-stabilised**.

In concrete terms, what does this change? A conventional measuring scale expands slightly with
heat — the workshop temperature, the heat generated by the machine itself during machining.
On a high-precision part, even a variation of a few tenths of a degree can introduce geometric
drift.

Thermo-stabilisation neutralises this effect: **the machine measures accurately regardless of
ambient temperature**, maintaining its precision throughout the entire machining cycle — whether
one hour or ten. For tight-tolerance parts or long runs, this is a repeatability guarantee
that very few machines can offer.

## Cut F 350 vs Cut E 600: two complementary machines

| | Cut F 350 | Cut E 600 |
|---|---|---|
| Travel X × Y | 350 × 250 mm | 600 × 400 mm |
| Strength | Performance · Precision · Finish | Large travel · Heavy load |
| Generator | Next-generation | Standard high-end |
| Thermo-stabilisation | Yes | No |
| Max. table load | 500 kg | 1,000 kg |

The two machines cover different needs. The Cut F 350 is the natural choice whenever
**surface quality, absolute precision or repeatability on a run** are the priority.
The **[Cut E 600](/en/articles/cut-e-600-technical-sheet)** takes over when the **part size or
its mass** exceeds common capacity.
