Welcome to the blog of **LIP Industrie Précision**, your partner in general and precision mechanics in
Besançon, France, for over 30 years.

## Why this blog?

This blog shares our expertise and answers the questions that design offices and industrial buyers ask
when looking for a precision machining subcontractor. You will find:

- **technical explanations** of our processes (CNC turning and milling, grinding, wire and ram EDM);
- **tips** to prepare your drawings and improve the machinability of your parts;
- our **news** and the sectors we serve (medical, aerospace, space, automotive, defence and more).

## An autonomous, ISO 9001 certified workshop

We produce finished parts, from single units to medium runs, in all ferrous and non-ferrous metals as
well as plastics. Our **ISO 9001** certification and our inspection equipment (coordinate measuring
machine, OGP optical camera) guarantee the conformity of every part we deliver.
