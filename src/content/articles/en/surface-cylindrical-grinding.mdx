Grinding is the reference finishing process in precision machining. Where milling or turning
leave a part within a few hundredths of the nominal, grinding goes down **to the micron** —
and delivers surface finishes that no other material-removal process can match as consistently.
At LIP Industrie Précision, we operate three types of grinding in-house: **surface**,
**cylindrical** and **centreless**.

## Surface grinding

<Image src={imgPlane} alt="Surface grinder with metal part on magnetic chuck — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Precision surface grinding — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" />

Surface grinding produces **perfectly flat and parallel surfaces** with very low Ra values.
The part is held on a **magnetic chuck** — a non-intrusive method that leaves all faces
accessible without mechanical clamping.

Our surface grinders handle parts up to **500 × 250 × 300 mm**. This covers a wide range
of workpieces: gauge blocks, dies, guide surfaces, datum surfaces, flanges, base plates.

<Image src={imgPiece} alt="Rectangular part after surface grinding on magnetic chuck — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Surface grinding — mirror finish on steel part, LIP Industrie Précision" />

Typical surface grinding applications:

- Strict **flatness and parallelism** on shims, spacers, plates.
- **Sliding surfaces** and guide surfaces with low Ra.
- **Dimensional correction** on worn or out-of-tolerance parts.
- **Die finishing** after heat treatment — grinding corrects the distortion induced by hardening.

## Cylindrical grinding

<Image src={imgCylindrique} alt="Cylindrical grinder with Molemab wheel and tailstock — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Cylindrical grinding — Molemab, LIP Industrie Précision" />

Cylindrical grinding works **surfaces of revolution**: shaft journals, bores, tapers,
shoulders. It is the essential process whenever a diameter dimension must be held to within
a few microns with a surface finish below Ra 0.4 µm.

<Image src={imgMeule} alt="Close-up of the Molemab grinding wheel on a cylindrical grinder — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Molemab cylindrical grinding wheel — LIP Industrie Précision" />

Our cylindrical grinder covers diameters up to **Ø 200 mm** and lengths up to **500 mm**.
It works between centres or in a chuck depending on the part.

Common applications:

- **Bearing seats**: H7/k6 fit or tighter, Ra 0.4 µm or better.
- **Drive shafts** and precision pins.
- **Hardened parts**: after hardening, grinding is often the only way to reach the final
  dimension without distortion risk.
- **Internal bores** by internal grinding.

## Centreless grinding

**Centreless** grinding is our tool for **small-diameter parts in production runs**. Without
a chuck or centres, the part is guided by a regulating wheel and a work rest blade — enabling
much higher throughput than conventional cylindrical grinding.

Our centreless grinder covers diameters from **Ø 2 to 40 mm**, ideal for:

- Pins, dowels, rods in large batches.
- **Connector** and **medical** parts with tight diameter tolerances.
- Bar stock calibration before machining.

## Why grind after machining or heat treatment?

This is a question design offices often ask: why not finish directly on the lathe or mill?
Two main reasons:

1. **Heat treatment distorts.** A part hardened to 60 HRC can no longer be cut by a
   conventional tool — grinding is the only process that effectively attacks very hard steels
   while holding final dimensions.

2. **Surface finish.** Even a careful turning pass rarely gets below Ra 0.8 µm. Grinding
   reaches Ra 0.2 µm or less in finishing — a level required for sealing surfaces, sliding
   contacts or medical parts.
