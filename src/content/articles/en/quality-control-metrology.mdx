The quality of a machined part is not assessed by eye — it is proven with numbers.
At LIP Industrie Précision, inspection is not an optional final step: it is a discipline
integrated into every batch, supported by a dedicated metrology equipment and an
**ISO 9001** certification maintained for many years. Here is the equipment that guarantees
the conformity of every part we deliver.

## Thomet Rapide coordinate measuring machine (CMM)

<Image src={imgTridimAction} alt="Renishaw TP8 probe of the Thomet Rapide CMM measuring a part — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Thomet Rapide CMM with Renishaw TP8 probe — quality control LIP Industrie Précision" />

Our **Thomet Rapide** coordinate measuring machine is the centrepiece of our metrology room.
Fitted with a **Renishaw TP8 probe**, it measures the complete three-dimensional geometry of a
part: positions, distances, diameters, perpendicularity, flatness, concentricity.

This type of inspection is essential whenever a drawing includes geometric dimensioning and
tolerancing (GD&T) that manual instruments — micrometers, dial gauges — cannot qualify alone.
The CMM approaches the part from every angle, records measurement points and generates a
complete, traceable inspection report.

In practice, it is used for:

- **First-off parts** of each batch to validate machine setup.
- **Complex parts** with many inter-related dimensions.
- **Critical parts** for the medical, space and aerospace sectors, where traceable inspection
  is required.

## OGP optical camera

<Image src={imgOGP} alt="OGP optical camera with VectorLight probe and dual measurement screens — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="OGP optical camera — non-contact measurement, quality control LIP Industrie Précision" />

The **OGP optical camera** (Optical Gaging Products) is our reference tool for
**non-contact measurement**. Using its vision system and **VectorLight probe**, it measures
contours, bores, radii and profiles of fragile parts or parts whose surface must not be touched.

It is particularly valuable for:

- **Thin or fragile parts** that a CMM probe could deform.
- **Small geometries** and micro-details that are difficult to reach mechanically.
- **Fast 2D checks** on batches: the camera digitises contours in seconds and compares them to
  the drawing.
- Parts from **wire EDM**, whose complex profiles are the process's speciality.

## Mitutoyo roughness tester

<Image src={imgRugo} alt="Mitutoyo roughness tester displaying Ra 0.354 µm on a machined part — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" title="Mitutoyo roughness tester — Ra surface finish measurement in µm, LIP Industrie Précision" />

**Surface finish** is a full specification in its own right on many drawings: Ra 0.8 µm,
Ra 1.6 µm, Ra 3.2 µm… Our **Mitutoyo roughness tester** measures these values directly on the
part, with a resolution of one hundredth of a micron.

This is a systematic check on every part where an Ra value is specified — whether it is a
sliding surface, a sealing seat or a medical surface finish. The result is numerical, recorded
and irrefutable.

## The rest of our inspection equipment

The CMM and the OGP are our flagship instruments, but our metrology room also includes:

- **Height gauges** for steps and shoulders.
- **Profile projectors** for complex contours seen in silhouette.
- **Dial gauges** and **micrometers** for rapid in-process checks.
- **Gauge blocks** for periodic instrument verification.

All of this equipment is maintained and calibrated regularly as part of our **ISO 9001**
quality management system.

A complete metrology setup is a concrete guarantee: every part delivered has been measured,

incoming inspection to hope for — the part is good when it leaves us.

That is what we mean by **"The guarantee of a precise response"**.
