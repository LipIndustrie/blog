At LIP Industrie Précision, the workshop runs on a simple principle: **the expert knows their
work better than anyone else**. This is not a slogan — it is a concrete organisation that has
structured the way we work every day for more than 30 years.

## One technician, one sector, one responsibility

Each technician **owns their sector**. The Variaxis milling operator knows their machine in
every detail: its thermal behaviour, its limits, its optimal machining strategies. The EDM
specialist masters their wire parameters and sinker settings. The turning department manager
validates his own parts on the CMM.

This is not delegation by default — it is a deliberate choice. When someone knows their tool
perfectly, they detect drift before it becomes a problem, they adapt their strategies without
waiting for an instruction, they guarantee consistent quality from the first to the last part.
Sector ownership produces a level of quality that supervision alone cannot reach.

## Autonomy as a working method

The philosophy is consistent: **if we trust a technician's expertise, we trust them to
organise their own work**. No micro-management of method, no procedures that override
on-the-floor judgement. The technician has the information, the tools and the freedom to
organise that they need to work effectively.

In practice, this means:
- Full access to processes and manufacturing data.
- Work organisation driven by the real constraints of the machine and the parts in progress.
- Setup and strategy decisions made at the level of the person holding the tool.

This way of working does not suit everyone. It demands genuine technical mastery, the ability
to anticipate, and a commitment to doing good work for its own sake — not because someone is
watching.

## Training: manufacturers and editors as partners

Developing expertise requires training — and we do not limit ourselves to in-house instruction.
Our technicians are trained **directly by the manufacturers and software editors**: **Mazak**
on machining centres and Smooth Technology programming, **+GF+ (AgieCharmilles)** on EDM
machines, **Topsolid** on CAD/CAM. The people who design the machines are the ones who pass
on their mastery.

This approach has a concrete effect: our technicians do not just know their machines'
functions — they understand the underlying logic, the manufacturer's limits, the possibilities
that standard documentation does not always cover.

Training is not a one-off event. It is maintained and evolves with the machines, the
materials, and the demands of the parts being produced.

## What this means for our clients

For a client, this organisation has a direct consequence: **every process is held by someone
who masters it end to end**. Not an operator following a routing sheet, but an expert who
understands why the routing is written that way — and who can adapt it when the part requires
it.

This is particularly visible on complex or critical parts: those combining several processes,
those requiring tight tolerances on difficult geometry, those where a setup error shows up at
final inspection. In these cases, the quality of the result depends directly on the depth of
the operator's expertise.

It is also what allows us to work for demanding sectors — medical, aerospace, space,
connector — where traceability and repeatability are not optional.

## A business built to last

More than 30 years of activity in Besançon, ISO 9001 certification, clients who come back —
this is not the result of a sales strategy. It is the result of consistent work, done
seriously, by people who know what they are doing.

The experts' workshop is not a tagline. It is a description of how we operate.
