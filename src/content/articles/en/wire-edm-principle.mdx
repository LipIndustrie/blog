**Wire EDM** (electrical discharge machining, or wire cutting) is a precision machining process that
cuts metal with no mechanical contact and no cutting force. It produces complex shapes, sharp corners
and tight tolerances, even on very hard materials. Here is how it works, the wire types we master and
its main applications.

## The principle: cutting metal with sparks

Unlike turning or milling, wire EDM does not remove material with a cutting tool. It uses a **series
of electrical discharges** (sparks) between a **conductive wire** and the workpiece, both immersed in
a **dielectric** (deionised water).

<Image src={cuttingHead} alt="Cutting head of a wire EDM machine with its flushing nozzles and the wire tensioned above the worktable" title="Wire EDM: cutting head and flushing nozzles — LIP Industrie Précision, Besançon" />

In practice:

1. A thin metal wire (often **0.25 mm** in diameter, sometimes much less) runs continuously between
   two guides.
2. An electrical voltage creates **micro-sparks** that locally melt and vaporise the metal.
3. The dielectric flushes away the particles and cools the cutting zone.
4. The part (or the wire) moves along the programmed path: the wire "cuts" the required shape, like a
   cheese wire but at micron scale.

Because there is **no mechanical force**, thin, fragile or already heat-treated parts can be machined
without distortion.

## Why choose wire EDM?

- **Hard materials**: it machines all conductive metals, including hardened steels, carbide, titanium
  and stainless steel — regardless of hardness.
- **Precision and surface finish**: tolerances of a few microns and sharp corners that milling
  cannot achieve.
- **Complex shapes**: internal contours, tapered profiles, small radii.
- **No distortion**: ideal for thin or sensitive parts.

## The wires we master

The wire choice directly affects cutting speed, surface finish and part cleanliness. At LIP Industrie
Précision we work with a **wide range of wires** and always select the one best suited to your
material, the target precision and your cleanliness requirements. Here are a few representative
examples of that versatility:

### Brass wire (standard)

Brass wire is the reference for most jobs: a good balance between cutting speed and cost. It suits
most steels and everyday applications.

### 100% copper, zinc-free wire (no deposit)

For **contamination-sensitive** applications we use a **100% copper, zinc-free** wire. The absence of
zinc prevents any **deposit** on the part — a critical point for **medical**, **space** or any part
requiring metallurgical cleanliness.

### Molybdenum (Moly) wire

**Molybdenum** wire offers very high tensile strength and allows **very fine diameters**. It is
valuable for micro-cutting and the most delicate details. It is also a go-to choice when **brass or
zinc deposits are a problem**: free of these metals, it prevents any contamination of the part — a
decisive advantage for applications where metallurgical cleanliness is critical.

Beyond these examples, we stock and run **many other wire types** (coated, zinc-coated, high-speed,
various diameters…) depending on each part's requirements. This diversity is at the heart of our
**adaptability**: rather than forcing a single wire, we fit the tool to your specification.

## In summary

Wire EDM is the process of choice whenever you need to **machine a hard material with high precision**
or obtain shapes that no cutting tool can produce. The wire choice (brass, zinc-free copper,
molybdenum) depends on the part, the precision and the cleanliness requirements.
