## Quick reference

- A full turn is **360°**, i.e. **2π radians** (≈ 6.2832) or **400 gradians**.
- The **gradian** (gon) divides the quarter circle into 100 parts: 90° = 100 gon. It is mainly used
  in surveying.
- The **radian** is the SI unit; 1 rad ≈ 57.2958°.
- In **DMS**, 1° = 60 arcminutes (′) and 1′ = 60 arcseconds (″). Example: 12.5° = 12° 30′ 0″.
