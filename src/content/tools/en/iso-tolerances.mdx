## How to read the table

- **es / ei**: upper and lower deviations of the shaft (in µm, algebraic).  
  *Max shaft Ø = nominal Ø + es/1000 ; Min shaft Ø = nominal Ø + ei/1000.*
- **EI / ES**: lower and upper deviations of the hole (in µm, algebraic).  
  *Min hole Ø = nominal Ø + EI/1000 ; Max hole Ø = nominal Ø + ES/1000.*
- **IT**: tolerance value (IT = es − ei = ES − EI), in µm.
- **Max clearance** = ES hole − ei shaft ; **Min clearance** = EI hole − es shaft.  
  A negative min clearance means **guaranteed interference**.

## ISO 286-1 key points

- The tolerance zone position is defined by the **fundamental deviation** (letter):
  lowercase for shafts (`h`, `f`, `p`…), uppercase for holes (`H`, `F`, `P`…).
- **h / H** are the reference positions (zero deviation on the material side): shaft `h` and
  hole `H` are the bases of the basic-shaft and basic-hole fit systems.
- The **IT grade** defines the width of the tolerance zone: IT5 (very precise) → IT12 (coarse).
- Values are calculated per ISO 286-1:2010 tables for diameters from 1 to 500 mm.

## Common fits — quick selection guide

| Fit | Typical use |
|---|---|
| H7/f7 | Sliding with noticeable clearance — shaft rotating in bronze bearing |
| H7/g6 | Precision sliding — cylinder rod, drill shank |
| H7/h6 | Zero-clearance location — removable assembly |
| H7/k6 | Near-zero / light interference — precise, repeatable location |
| H7/n6 | Light press fit — light press or heating required |
| H7/p6 | Standard press fit — permanent assembly by press |
| H7/s6 | Heavy press fit — non-demountable, drive shaft |
| H8/f7 | Free-running — spindle, standard ball bearing |
| H11/c11 | Free — parts in dusty environments |
